## Introduction

WorkChief is an AI-powered operations management platform designed to transform daily team updates into actionable insights. It helps executives, HR managers, and team leaders:

- **Track team performance** with automated daily check-ins
- **Detect blockers** before they become critical issues
- **Generate AI-powered analysis** with performance scoring
- **Create executive briefings** with actionable next steps

---

## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

1. Navigate to [workchief.ai](https://workchief.ai)
2. Click **"Get Started"** or **"Sign In"**
3. Choose your authentication method:
   - Email and password signup
   - Google OAuth (Sign in with Google)
4. Complete the registration process

### First Login

Upon first login, you'll be directed to:
- **CEO/HR/Executive Assistant**: The Dashboard for team overview
- **Team Members**: The Reports page for submitting daily updates

## User Roles

WorkChief supports four distinct user roles:

| Role | Access Level | Capabilities |
|------|--------------|--------------|
| **CEO** | Full access | All features, team management, analytics, permissions |
| **HR** | Management access | Team management, check-ins, analytics, reports |
| **Executive Assistant** | Management access | Team management, check-ins, analytics, reports |
| **Team Member** | Limited access | Submit metrics, view own reports |

### Role-Based Navigation

- **CEO/HR/EA**: Dashboard, Team, Check-ins, Metrics, Analysis, Analytics, Reports, Permissions, Settings
- **Team Members**: Metrics, Reports, Settings

## Dashboard

The Dashboard provides a bird's-eye view of team performance.

### CEO Dashboard Components

1. **Summary Cards**
   - Total team members
   - Green performance percentage (healthy teams)
   - Active blockers count
   - Today's check-ins submitted

2. **Recent Activity**
   - Latest check-ins with status indicators
   - Performance scores (🟢 Green / 🟡 Yellow / 🔴 Red)
   - Quick access to detailed analysis

3. **Performance Breakdown**
   - Team-wide performance distribution
   - Department-level insights

### Status Indicators

- 🟢 **Green**: Performing well, no blockers
- 🟡 **Yellow**: Minor issues, needs attention
- 🔴 **Red**: Critical blockers, immediate action required

## Team Management

### Adding Team Members

1. Navigate to **Team** page
2. Click **"Add Member"**
3. Fill in the required information:
   - Name
   - Email address
   - Role/Position
   - Department
   - Target metrics (optional)
4. Click **Save**

### Editing Team Members

1. Find the team member card
2. Click the **Edit** (pencil) icon
3. Update information as needed
4. Click **Save**

### Removing Team Members

1. Find the team member card
2. Click the **Delete** (trash) icon
3. Confirm deletion in the dialog

### Linking Team Members to Accounts

Team members can be linked to user accounts, allowing them to:
- Log in with their own credentials
- Submit daily metrics and reports
- View their personal performance history

## Daily Check-ins

### Processing Check-ins (CEO/HR/EA)

1. Navigate to **Check-ins** page
2. View the **Reports Panel** showing today's submitted reports
3. Click **"Use"** on a report to auto-populate the check-in form
4. Select the team member (auto-selected when using a report)
5. Review/edit the notes and metrics
6. Click **"Analyze & Submit"**

### AI Analysis Output

After submission, the AI generates:
- **Performance Score**: Green/Yellow/Red status
- **Message**: Summary of performance
- **Reason**: Explanation for the score
- **Blockers**: Identified issues preventing progress
- **Next Steps**: Recommended actions

## Metrics Tracking

### Team Member Metrics Page

Team members can submit their daily metrics:

1. Navigate to **Metrics** page
2. View your assigned target metrics
3. Enter actual values for each metric
4. Add any notes or comments
5. Click **Submit**

### CEO Metrics Dashboard

Managers can view all team metrics:

1. Navigate to **Metrics** page
2. View real-time submission status
3. See "Submitted" or "Report not submitted" badges
4. Export data to PDF for records

## Reports

### Submitting Reports (Team Members)

1. Navigate to **Reports** page
2. Write your daily update in the text area
3. Include:
   - What you accomplished today
   - Any blockers or challenges
   - Plans for tomorrow
4. Click **Submit Report**

### Viewing Reports (Managers)

1. Navigate to **Reports** or **Check-ins** page
2. View all submitted reports for the day
3. Use reports to process check-ins

## Permissions Management

### Assigning Roles (CEO Only)

1. Navigate to **Permissions** page
2. View current role assignments
3. Assign HR or Executive Assistant roles:
   - Only one person can hold each role at a time
   - Previous holder must be removed first
4. Confirm the assignment

### Role Hierarchy

```
CEO (Owner)
├── HR (1 person max)
├── Executive Assistant (1 person max)
└── Team Members (unlimited)
```

## FAQs

### Q: How often should team members submit reports?

**A:** Daily submissions are recommended for accurate performance tracking and timely blocker detection.

### Q: Can I use WorkChief on mobile devices?

**A:** Yes! WorkChief is fully responsive and works on all modern browsers and devices.

### Q: How does the AI scoring work?

**A:** The AI analyzes submitted metrics and notes against targets, considering:
- Goal completion percentage
- Identified blockers
- Historical performance trends
- Context from notes

### Q: Who can see my reports?

**A:** Reports are visible to:
- CEO (all reports)
- HR and Executive Assistants (all reports)
- The team member who submitted it

### Q: How do I reset my password?

**A:** On the login page, click "Forgot Password" and follow the email instructions.

### Q: Can I export my data?

**A:** Yes, the Metrics page includes a PDF export feature for reports and analytics.
